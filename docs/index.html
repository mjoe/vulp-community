Internet radio in your macOS menu bar

Vulp plays thousands of internet radio streams right from the menu bar — with search, favorites, recording, live playback and Now Playing support. Easy on the battery, beautiful in the icon bar.

Get Vulp

Vulp requires macOS 26 or newer and is distributed through the Mac App Store, with a yearly subscription (CHF 6) including a free 7-day trial.

New streams are discovered through the radio-browser.info directory, so you can search for stations by genre, country or language directly in the app.

Features

  • Menu bar playbackKeep the stream visible in the menu bar with an animated custom icon — split-rotate on play, slow-fill on pause.
  • Radio browserSearch the radio-browser.info directory and add stations to your favorites with one click.
  • Favorites & editingManage your playlists, edit stream details and import/export your library as YAML.
  • RecordingRecord your favorite shows locally as M4A with embedded artwork, saved into your Music folder.
  • Now PlayingFull Media Center integration — control playback from Control Center, the keyboard or Siri.
  • Call & meeting awareVulp notices when a call or meeting is active and pauses automatically, then resumes afterwards.
  • Live artwork & metadataStream logos and song titles update live in the menu bar and in the widget.
  • Widget & iCloud syncA Home-Screen widget shows what's playing; your library syncs across your own Macs via iCloud (optional).

Privacy

Vulp stores your stream list locally on your Mac. If you enable Cloud Sync, your library is synchronized through your personal iCloud account. Recordings stay on your machine in your Music folder. Vulp does not use analytics or third-party ad tracking.

Recording is for personal use only — please respect each station's terms of service.

Support & Feedback

  • Report a bugDescribe the problem, your macOS version and the steps to reproduce it in a bug report. Attach screenshots if you can.
